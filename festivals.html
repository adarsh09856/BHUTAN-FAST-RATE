Sacred Buddhist Celebrations

Bhutan Festival Calendar 2027

Witness centuries-old Cham masked dances performed by revered Buddhist masters, silk thongdrel blessings, and royal celebrations across the Kingdom.

2027 Official Schedule

Grand Tshechus of Bhutan

Tshechu translates to "tenth day", celebrating Guru Padmasambhava who brought Mahayana Buddhism to the Himalayas in the 8th century.

Sep 19–21, 2027

Thimphu Tshechu

Three days of transcendent masked dances at Tashichho Dzong courtyard honouring Guru Rinpoche with royal presence.

Mar 28 – Apr 1, 2027

Paro Tshechu

Witness the pre-dawn unveiling of the four-story sacred Guru Thongdrel silk scroll that liberates onlookers by sight alone.

Feb 14–18, 2027

Punakha Drubchen

Dramatic battlefield re-enactment honoring Zhabdrung Ngawang Namgyal's victory over invading Tibetan armies in the 1600s.

Oct 24–28, 2027

Jambay Lhakhang Drup

Famous for the sacred midnight fire blessing (Mewang) where devotees sprint beneath fiery archways for purification.

Festival Courtyard Etiquette & Insider Tips

👗 Dress Modestly

Long trousers or skirts, collared shirts, and covered shoulders are strictly required. Wearing a traditional Kira or Gho is warmly welcomed by locals.

📸 Photography Respect

Cameras are permitted in courtyard areas, but strictly prohibited inside the inner monastery sanctums. Never interrupt dancers during sacred Cham movements.

🎟️ VIP Courtyard Seating

All Bhutan Fast Rate Tours travelers enjoy pre-reserved shaded cushion seating in the official guest terrace, ensuring uninterrupted views and comfort.
