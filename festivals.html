Cultural Respect & Guidelines

Festival Etiquette in the Kingdom

As a guest at a sacred Buddhist ceremony, observing proper decorum ensures a welcoming and meaningful experience.

1

Modest Attire

Shoulders, knees, and ankles must be covered. Collared shirts or formal coats are customary. Shorts, hats, and revealing clothes are strictly prohibited inside the dzong courtyard.

2

Photography Etiquette

Photography is welcomed during the outdoor courtyard dances. However, photography inside the inner temple sanctums and of certain secret thangkas is not permitted.

3

No Smoking or Alcohol

Smoking is illegal in public and religious areas throughout Bhutan. Maintain quiet and reverent demeanor during monastic blessing processions.

4

The Atsaras (Clowns)

The masked jesters (Atsaras) mock societal illusions and sexual taboos with wooden phalluses. They are revered as enlightened masters testing your composure—smile and enjoy!
