Understanding Bhutan's SDF Fee

The Sustainable Development Fee (SDF) supports Bhutan's free healthcare, free education, carbon-negative ecology, and cultural heritage preservation.

✓ US $100 per adult per night (reduced 50% from former $200 rate).
✓ Children aged 6–12 receive 50% concession (US $50 per night).
✓ Children under 6 are completely exempt from SDF fees.
✓ Indian Passport Holders: Nu. 1,200 (approx ₹1,200) per person/night.
