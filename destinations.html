Sacred Himalayan Geography

Bhutan Destinations & Valleys Guide

Discover the unique character, microclimates, monasteries, and fortress dzongs of each region across the Land of the Thunder Dragon.

Western Bhutan • 2,280m Elevation

Paro Valley

The historic gateway to the Kingdom. Paro is crowned by the cliff-hanging Taktsang (Tiger's Nest) Monastery, the 15th-century Rinpung Dzong, and the 7th-century Kyichu Lhakhang. Paro's fertile valley floor produces world-renowned red rice and crisp apples.

Highlights: Tiger's Nest Hike • National Museum (Ta Dzong) • Traditional Farmhouse Bath
The Capital • 2,334m Elevation

Thimphu Valley

The only world capital with zero traffic lights, directed by white-gloved traffic officers with elegant balletic hand movements. Thimphu blends ancient Buddhist traditions with modern arts, housing the 169ft golden Buddha Dordenma, Tashichho Dzong, and the Royal Textile Academy.

Highlights: Buddha Dordenma • Centenary Farmers Market • Motithang Takin Preserve
Winter Capital • 1,300m Elevation

Punakha & Wangdue

A lush subtropical valley blessed with warm breezes and banana trees. Punakha Dzong, the Palace of Great Happiness, sits at the confluence of the Pho Chhu (Male) and Mo Chhu (Female) rivers. Cross the 160m suspension bridge and hike through terraced fields to Chimi Lhakhang.

Highlights: Punakha Dzong • Mo Chhu Rafting • Chimi Lhakhang Fertility Temple
Glacial Sanctuary • 3,000m Elevation

Phobjikha Glacial Valley

One of the few glacial valleys in Bhutan. An expansive U-shaped wilderness bordered by the Black Mountains. It serves as the sacred winter roosting sanctuary for over 500 endangered Black-necked Cranes, watched over by the hillside Gangtey Goempa monastery.

Highlights: Black-necked Crane Sanctuary • Gangtey Nature Trail • Alpine Fireplace Lodges
