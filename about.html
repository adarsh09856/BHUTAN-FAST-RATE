About Bhutan Fast Rate Tours

Founded in Thimphu by lifelong Himalayan mountaineers and cultural scholars. Discover our founding philosophy, certified guide team, and commitment to carbon-negative Gross National Happiness travel.

Our Heritage & Ethos

High Value, Low Impact Tourism

Bhutan Fast Rate Tours was established with a singular mission: to open the gates of our sacred Himalayan home to curious world travelers without compromising the pristine ecology, ancient traditions, or spiritual sanctity of the Kingdom.

Bhutan is the world's only carbon-negative nation — over 72% of our land is carpeted in virgin forests. Every journey booked with us directly contributes to the Royal Government's free universal healthcare, education, and ecological stewardship programs.

Guiding Philosophy

The 4 Pillars of Gross National Happiness

1. Sustainable Socio-Economic Development

Tourism revenue directly funds local schools, hospitals, and organic farming co-operatives in rural dzongkhags.

2. Cultural Preservation

Supporting the Zorig Chusum (13 Traditional Arts), sacred cham masked dances, and monastic restoration.

3. Environmental Conservation

Strict zero-plastic policies on trekking routes and 100% carbon-offset emissions for all ground transport.

4. Good Governance

Transparent, audited operations certified by the Department of Tourism and the Association of Bhutanese Tour Operators.
