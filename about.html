Our Purpose

Tourism That Nourishes the Kingdom

Bhutan has never subscribed to mass commercial tourism. Since 1974, our nation has operated under the visionary policy of "High Value, Low Volume".

At Bhutan Fast Rate Tours, our mission is to ensure every dollar you spend directly benefits Bhutan’s free healthcare, free public education, and over 70% forest cover preservation. We employ local storytellers, support generational weaver families, and select farmsteads that practice zero-waste organic farming.

The Philosophy of Bhutan

Guided by Gross National Happiness (GNH)

GNH is not a myth or marketing slogan; it is the constitutional foundation of Bhutanese life. We embody these four pillars in every itinerary we curate:

1

Cultural Preservation

Protecting sacred Buddhist rituals, architectural codes, traditional dress, and centuries-old handicrafts from global homogenization.

2

Environmental Conservation

By constitutional mandate, 60% of Bhutan must remain under forest cover forever (currently 72%). Bhutan absorbs three times more carbon than it emits.

3

Equitable Socio-Economic Growth

Tourism revenue supports rural valley livelihoods, universal free healthcare, and free higher education across all 20 dzongkhags.

4

Good Governance

Leadership founded on ethical responsibility, citizen well-being, and community harmony over unchecked commercial exploitation.
