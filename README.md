# BHUTAN-FAST-RATE

Official Tourism, Cultural Expeditions & Luxury Himalayan Journeys in the Kingdom of Bhutan.

## Overview
Certified by the Department of Tourism, Royal Government of Bhutan (License #DOT-2024-884). Handcrafted itineraries, high Himalayan treks, 2027 festival calendars, and interactive Sustainable Development Fee (SDF) calculator.

## Key Features
- **100% Device-Friendly**: Zero overlapping elements, clean responsive grid and flexbox layout across mobile (320px–480px), iPad/tablet (768px), and desktop.
- **Interactive SDF Fee Calculator**: Real-time government fee calculation ($100/night international vs Nu. 1,200 Indian passport holders, child concessions, and exemptions).
- **Curated Tour Packages**: 8 comprehensive itineraries with day-by-day interactive modals (Taktsang Tiger's Nest, Punakha Dzong, Druk Path Trek, Amankora Luxury Lodges, Festivals, Honeymoon Romance).
- **Festivals Calendar 2027**: Official Cham masked dance schedule, Tashichho Dzong courtyard seating, and booking guides.
- **Drukair & Bhutan Airlines Ticketing**: Direct seat allocations to Paro International Airport (PBH).
