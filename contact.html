Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know Before Visiting

How difficult is the Tiger’s Nest (Taktsang) hike?

The hike takes roughly 4 to 5 hours round-trip. It is a moderate uphill trail through fragrant pine forests. For guests who prefer not to walk the full ascent, we arrange gentle horseback / pony rides up to the halfway cafeteria viewpoint.

Is Bhutan safe for solo or female travelers?

Bhutan is consistently ranked as one of the safest nations on Earth. Violent crime is virtually non-existent, and Buddhist cultural values emphasize mutual respect and hospitality. You are accompanied by a dedicated certified guide and driver.

What currency is accepted in Bhutan?

The national currency is the Bhutanese Ngultrum (Nu.), which is pegged 1:1 to the Indian Rupee (INR). Indian Rupees are widely accepted everywhere. Major international credit cards are accepted at all luxury hotels and handicraft emporiums.

Can I customize any of your standard tour routes?

Absolutely. Every tour can be customized with extra days, preferred boutique or 5-star lodges (Amankora / Six Senses / Pemako), private monk blessings, photography expeditions, or helicopter transfers.
